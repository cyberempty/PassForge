Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2024

1. Information Collection

PassForge is a client-side password generator that runs entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or passwords generated by this tool.

2. Data Storage

All password generation happens locally on your device. No data is sent to any server or stored externally. Your passwords remain on your device.

3. Cookies and Tracking

PassForge does not use cookies or any tracking technologies. We do not track your usage or collect any analytics data.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts for typography. These services may collect basic usage information according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Security

While we implement best practices for password generation, we recommend using a reputable password manager for storing your passwords securely.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our website.
